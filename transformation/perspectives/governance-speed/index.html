Perspectives · Governance

Governance is what lets you move fast

Controls added afterwards slow every project down. Controls built into the platform let a large organization say yes with confidence.

AL Ops · Published 30 September 2026

Organizations often move slowly with AI not for lack of ideas, but because nobody can answer basic questions with confidence: what is running, who approved it, what can it touch, and what did it do?

When governance is a project, every use case starts from zero

If each workflow needs its own security review, audit design and approval path, the tenth costs as much as the first, and risk teams re-decide the same questions without shared evidence.

Build the controls once, into the platform

  • An inventory of AI assets with owners, versions and risk class, including anything discovered but not yet approved.
  • An identity for every agent with least-privilege access, so every action is attributable.
  • Policy enforced outside the agent, with a deny-by-default posture and the ability to suspend an agent.
  • Checks at each step for injected instructions, data leakage and policy violations.
  • Approvals scaled to risk, with consequential or irreversible actions always reaching a person.
  • A record that reconstructs a run, from request to context, tools, approvals and result.

When controls are part of the platform, approving the tenth workflow is a review of what is different, not a restart.

Certification as a predictable path

A defined route from development through evaluation, security review and business approval gives teams a known list of evidence and gives risk teams one place to review it.

Controls that move with you

Controls that live in the platform, not in one vendor's product, stay with you when the technology changes. That is what lets an organization adopt the next technology without rebuilding its governance.
