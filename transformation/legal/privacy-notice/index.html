Website privacy notice.

How this website handles information about visitors.

Draft. This page is a framework prepared for counsel review. It is not in force and does not describe current contractual commitments.

What this site collects

This draft site does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. It stores one preference in your browser, your chosen colour theme, and that preference never leaves your device.

Forms

The architecture review and documentation request forms ask for your work email, organization, role and details of the workflow you want to discuss. In this draft build, form delivery is not configured and nothing you enter is sent or stored.

When delivery is enabled, this notice will state where submissions go, who can read them, how long they are kept and how to ask for their deletion. Updates are sent only if you tick the separate, optional box.

Customer data

Data processed for enterprise customers through the platform is covered by the data processing addendum, not by this notice.

Your privacy choices

This website does not sell or share personal information, does not use it for targeted advertising and sets no tracking cookies, so there is nothing to opt out of here. The only stored preference is your colour theme, kept in your browser. Clearing your browser storage removes it.

Your rights by region

Your rights depend on where you are and which law applies. The summaries below are a draft for counsel review and will be completed with the verified company and contact information.

United States

State privacy laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the CPRA, give residents rights to know what personal information is collected, to delete and correct it, to opt out of its sale or sharing, to limit the use of sensitive personal information, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. You may use an authorized agent. This site does not sell or share personal information.

Europe (EU, EEA and UK)

Under the GDPR and the UK GDPR you can ask for access to your personal data, its correction or erasure, restriction of processing and data portability; you can object to processing based on legitimate interests and withdraw consent at any time. You can complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner’s Office). Transfers outside the EEA or UK use recognised safeguards. Details of any EU or UK representative will be published here.

Middle East

Data protection laws in the region include the UAE Personal Data Protection Law, the DIFC Data Protection Law 2020, the ADGM Data Protection Regulations 2021, the Saudi Arabia Personal Data Protection Law and Qatar’s Law No. 13 of 2016. Rights generally include being informed, access, correction, erasure and objection, and these laws set conditions on transferring personal data outside the country. Data residency for customer deployments is agreed per deployment.

India

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 you can obtain a summary of the personal data processed about you, ask for its correction, completion, updating or erasure, withdraw consent, nominate a person to exercise your rights, and use grievance redressal before approaching the Data Protection Board of India. Grievance officer details will be published here.

Contact

Privacy contact details will be published with the verified company information. Until then, use the contact form and choose your region.
